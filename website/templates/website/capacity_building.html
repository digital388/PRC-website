Capacity Building Programक्षमता अभिवृद्धि कार्यक्रम

Building Capable Legislatorsसक्षम विधायक निर्माण

PRC's Capacity Building Program works with the Gandaki Provincial Assembly to strengthen women MPs and MPs from marginalized groups. Delivered under UNDP Nepal's Parliament Support Project (PSP), the programme concentrates on four things that make a legislator effective: the skills to do the job, the analysis that improves bills, sustained mentorship, and exchange with peers across provinces.

सक्षम विधायक निर्माण

पोखरा रिसर्च सेन्टरको क्षमता अभिवृद्धि कार्यक्रमले गण्डकी प्रदेश सभाका महिला सांसद तथा अल्पसङ्ख्यक समुदायका सांसदहरूको क्षमता सुदृढ गर्दै आएको छ। संयुक्त राष्ट्रसंघीय विकास कार्यक्रम (UNDP) नेपालको संसदीय सहयोग परियोजना (PSP) अन्तर्गत सञ्चालित यस कार्यक्रमले सांसदलाई प्रभावकारी बनाउने चार पक्षमा केन्द्रित छ: आवश्यक सीप, विधेयक सुधार गर्ने विश्लेषण, निरन्तर मार्गदर्शन र प्रदेशहरूबीचको अनुभव आदानप्रदान।

What We Focus Onहाम्रो केन्द्रबिन्दु

Skill Developmentसीप विकास

Need-based, expert-led trainings that give MPs the practical skills to legislate, scrutinise budgets and represent their constituents with confidence. Training topics are chosen from a rapid need assessment and the Assembly's live agenda.

  • Two half-day, non-residential trainings in each programme cycle
  • At least 25 MPs per event, with gender-balanced experts and participants
  • Thematic and technical skills: law making, oversight, representation and emerging policy issues

Bill Analysisविधेयक विश्लेषण

Research support for parliamentary committees so that laws are reviewed on evidence, not assumption. PRC mobilises thematic experts to scrutinise a selected bill and to analyse policy from a gender and human rights (GHRIA) perspective.

  • Review of amendment notices, gaps and drafting issues in the selected bill
  • Alignment with the Constitution of Nepal, federal laws and judicial precedents
  • Consultations with CSOs, research institutions and MPs, followed by a sharing meeting and a final review report
  • One policy brief analysing a provincial policy or bill from a GHRIA perspective

Mentorshipमार्गदर्शन

Sustained group mentoring for a small cohort of MPs, built around the issues their province actually faces. Rather than one-off events, the programme supports the same mentees across a series of sessions and tracks their progress.

  • Up to 10 mentees selected from the need assessment
  • Five group sessions of 2–3 hours on province-specific issues
  • Expert-facilitated discussions, exercises and peer problem-solving
  • Progress tracked after every session, with case studies capturing change

Inter-Province Exchangeअन्तर-प्रदेश अनुभव आदानप्रदान

A community of practice that connects mentees across Nepal's provinces, so that MPs learn from peers facing similar challenges and can advocate together on shared agendas.

  • Virtual community-of-practice meetings, with each partner leading a session in turn
  • Participation in partner-led sessions from other provinces
  • An in-person, 2-night/3-day inter-provincial sharing programme
  • Up to 10 participants from each provincial assembly, building a lasting peer network
